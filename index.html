MYRHR

Our first product is MYRHR (say “mirror”), a dream journal for iPhone. You speak or type a dream when you wake. Over time, MYRHR shows you the symbols and patterns that keep returning, and asks you about them. It never tells you what a dream means. That part is yours.

The words of your dreams are encrypted on your phone before they go anywhere. There are no ads and no tracking, and we do not sell data.

MYRHR is in private beta now.
